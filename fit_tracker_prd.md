# PRD: Amit Fat Loss & Triglyceride Reduction Tracker App

## 1. Product Name

**FitTriglyceride Tracker**

Working name can be changed later.

---

## 2. Product Purpose

This application is a personal health, fat-loss, and triglyceride-reduction tracker for Amit.

The app should help the user follow a structured 12-week plan to:

- Reduce weight from **90 kg to 80–82 kg**
- Reduce waist from **38 inches to 34–35 inches**
- Reduce triglycerides from **250–300 mg/dL toward below 180 mg/dL**
- Improve consistency with:
  - protein intake
  - walking
  - strength training
  - meal timing
  - sleep
  - no biscuits/sweets/namkeen
  - light late dinner

The app is not a medical diagnostic tool. It should work as a checklist, progress tracker, and intelligent coaching assistant.

## 4. Core User Problem

The user does not want a generic weight-loss plan. The user needs a practical application that tells him exactly:

- what to do today
- when to eat
- what to eat
- how much protein each meal gives
- whether he completed the day correctly
- whether his weight, waist, and triglyceride direction are improving
- what to change if results do not appear after 30 days

## 5. Product Goals

### Primary Goals

1. Daily checklist compliance
2. Fat-loss progress tracking
3. Triglyceride-risk reduction behavior tracking
4. Workout and walking adherence
5. Protein intake monitoring
6. Early plateau detection
7. Rule-based adjustment recommendations after 2–4 weeks

### Non-Goals

The app must not:

- Diagnose medical disease
- Change thyroid medication
- Prescribe medicines
- Replace a doctor
- Claim guaranteed triglyceride reduction

## 16. Adjustment Engine

The app should generate recommendations based on progress and compliance.

### Scenario 1: Weight loss below 2 kg after 30 days

Check compliance first.

If compliance is below 75%, recommend:

- improve checklist compliance before changing diet
- remove biscuit/sweet/namkeen slips
- complete 4 workouts/week
- complete office walk 3–4 days/week
- complete post-dinner walk daily

If compliance is above 75%, recommend:

- remove rice at dinner for 14 days
- keep dinner to protein + sabji + salad 4 nights/week
- increase average steps by 1,500/day
- reduce oil/nuts/hidden snacks
- ensure protein breakfast daily

### Scenario 2: Waist not reducing after 30 days

Recommend:

- stricter dinner rule
- no chapati + rice together
- no late snacking
- add 10-minute post-dinner walk daily
- increase strength workout quality
- track waist once weekly only, not daily

### Scenario 3: Protein below 80 g/day for 5 days in a week

Recommend one:

- add 1 scoop whey
- add 2 eggs
- add 200 g Greek curd
- add 100 g paneer/tofu
- add dal + curd combo

### Scenario 4: Steps below target for 4 days in a week

Recommend:

- 10-minute walk after lunch
- 10-minute post-dinner walk
- office walk at 5:30–6:15 PM at least 3 days/week
- walk during phone calls
- 5-minute walk every 90 minutes at work

### Scenario 5: Triglyceride-focused risk remains high

If user logs sweets/biscuits/namkeen/sugar drinks more than 2 times/week:

Recommend:

- zero biscuits for next 14 days
- zero sugar tea/coffee
- no fruit juice
- no namkeen
- replace evening snack with roasted chana/sprouts/whey/curd
- repeat lipid profile after 8–12 weeks with doctor

### Scenario 6: Energy low

Recommend:

- do not skip protein breakfast
- check sleep
- hydrate
- avoid fruit-only morning meal
- reduce workout intensity temporarily
- consider medical check if persistent fatigue

### Scenario 7: Sleep poor

Recommend:

- move sleep 15 minutes earlier every 4–5 days
- avoid intense night workout
- stop heavy AI/project work 30–45 minutes before sleep
- reduce late caffeine
- keep dinner lighter

## 17. Recommendation Templates

### Weekly Good Progress Message

> Good progress this week. Your weight and waist are moving in the right direction. Continue the same plan. Your main focus next week is to maintain protein above 95 g/day and complete office walk at least 3 times.

### Weekly Weak Progress Message

> Your result is below expected, but the app detected compliance gaps. Before reducing food further, fix the basics: no biscuits/sweets/namkeen, protein breakfast daily, and complete 3–4 office walks this week.

### 30-Day Plateau Message

> Your 30-day result is below target despite decent compliance. For the next 14 days, use the stricter version: no rice at dinner, protein + sabji dinner 4 nights/week, increase steps by 1,500/day, and check oil/nut portions.

### Medical Safety Message

> This app does not adjust thyroid medication or diagnose lipid disorders. Please consult your doctor for TSH, lipid profile, HbA1c, and medication decisions.

## 23. Final Product Vision

This app should act like a personal daily operating system for Amit's 12-week fat-loss and triglyceride-reduction plan.

It should not just store data. It should answer:

- Did I follow the plan today?
- Did I hit my protein target?
- Did I avoid the foods that increase triglyceride risk?
- Did I move enough?
- Is my waist reducing?
- Is my weight moving toward 80–82 kg?
- If not, what exact change should I make next week?

The app should be practical, strict, and result-oriented.
